In mathematics, **logic** is the study of reasoning. Rather than analyzing the content of a particular statement, logic is concerned with the *relationship* between statements and whether the reasoning is correct [1, p. 21].

For example:
- All mathematicians wear sandals.
- Anyone who wears sandals is an algebraist.
- All mathematicians are algebraists.

Logic is not concerned with whether or not any of these statements are necessarily true. Rather, it allows us to conclude that, if the first two statements are true, then the third statement must also be true [1, p. 21].
